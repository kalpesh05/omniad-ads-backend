## 2. Anti-AI Caption Linter & Humanizer

Automated linter checks on every Instagram caption:

### Forbidden Words / Banned AI Clichés
- **Flag and Replace**:
  - ❌ *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."* -> Start directly with the core problem.
  - ❌ *"Game-changer"*, *"Delve into"*, *"Tapestry"*, *"Beacon"*, *"Unlock the power"*, *"Harness"* -> Replace with plain spoken language.
  - ❌ Massive blocks of emojis (e.g. 🚀🔥💡🎯✨ in every paragraph) -> Limit to 1-2 intentional emojis per section.

### Layout & Formatting Rules
- **Line Breaks**: Max 2 sentences per paragraph block. Maintain whitespace for mobile scannability.
- **Hook in First Line**: The first line must stand alone and make the reader tap `...more`.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Single, focused CTA (e.g. "Comment 'GROWTH' and I'll DM you the link" or "Save this for your next launch").
- **Hashtag Balance**: Exactly 3 to 5 hyper-specific niche hashtags at the bottom, never 30 spam hashtags.

## 3. High-Converting Carousel Blueprint (10-Slide Max)

1. **Slide 1 (Cover)**: Big promise + high-contrast visual (Hook formula).
2. **Slide 2 (The Villain/Problem)**: Validate why conventional methods fail.
3. **Slides 3–7 (The Value Payload)**: 1 clear actionable insight per slide.
4. **Slide 8 (Summary Cheatsheet)**: Single diagram or consolidated list for easy screenshotting.
5. **Slide 9 (Call-to-Action)**: "Save this post for later. Share with a founder who needs this."
