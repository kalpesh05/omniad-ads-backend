# 12-Platform Paid Media & Ads Director Skill

Comprehensive audit, budget optimization, and creative performance intelligence across modern advertising channels (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, Amazon, Microsoft, Pinterest).

---

## 1. Unified Diagnostic KPIs & Health Thresholds

Across all ad channels, evaluate campaign health using these hard diagnostic formulas:

| Metric | Target Benchmark | Warning Threshold | Action to Take |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Hook Rate** (3s Views / Impressions) | **> 30%** | **< 20%** | Kill opening creative hook; test 3 new 0–3s variants. |
| **Hold Rate** (15s Views / 3s Views) | **> 35%** | **< 15%** | Script mid-video drops; cut dead air, speed up pacing. |
| **Click-Through-Rate (CTR)** | **> 1.8%** (Social) / **> 4.5%** (Search) | **< 0.9%** | Creative or audience mismatch; rewrite copy/headline. |
| **Cost Per Click (CPC)** | Platform dependent | Top 25% percentile | Audience saturation or low Quality Score. |
| **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** | **> 3.0x** (E-Com) / Target CAC (B2B) | **< Break-even** | Reallocate budget to winning ad sets; pause bleeders. |
| **Creative Fatigue Velocity** | Frequency < 2.5/week | **Frequency > 3.8 & CTR drops 25%** | Creative is fatigued. Rotate in fresh creative batch immediately. |

## 2. Platform-Specific Diagnostic Playbooks

### Google Ads Playbook
- **Performance Max (PMax) Hygiene**:
  - Audit asset groups to ensure "URL Expansion" is not cannibalizing branded search terms.
  - Apply negative keyword lists to PMax campaigns via Google Ads scripts or support.
- **Search Campaign Audit**:
  - Check Search Term Reports: eliminate broad match waste; move high-converting search terms to Exact Match.
  - Quality Score Audit: Ensure expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience score at least 7/10.

### Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) Playbook
- **Account Architecture**:
  - Consolidate fragmented ad sets into Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC) or broad CBO (Advantage Campaign Budget).
  - Test new creatives in a dedicated "Creative Sandbox" ad set; move proven winners into the CBO scaling campaign.
- **Fatigue Alert Protocol**:
  - When an ad has spent > 3x Target CPA without a conversion in 72 hours, auto-flag for pause.

### TikTok Ads Playbook
- **Spark Ads Advantage**: Always prefer Spark Ads (boosting native creator posts) over dark ads—Spark Ads typically deliver 25-40% higher completion rates.
- **Rotation Frequency**: TikTok creatives burn out in 10–14 days. Maintain a continuous pipeline of 3 new UGC variations per week.

### LinkedIn Ads (B2B) Playbook
- **Thought Leader Ads**: Sponsor personal posts from executives and founders rather than generic company page ads (boosts CTR by up to 2.5x).
- **Native Lead Gen Forms**: Use LinkedIn in-app forms with pre-filled profile data for mobile users to double lead conversion rates over external landing pages.

## 3. Autonomous Budget Reallocation Logic

When auditing multiple active campaigns:
1. **Identify the Bleeders**: Spend > \$100 with ROAS < 0.8x or 0 conversions $\to$ Flag to pause or reduce budget by 50%.
2. **Identify the Scalers**: ROAS > 3.5x with stable CPA over the last 7 days $\to$ Recommend increasing daily budget by **15% to 20%** (avoiding algorithm reset).
3. **Rebalance Cross-Platform**: If TikTok ROAS is dropping due to creative fatigue while Google Search ROAS is steady at 4.2x, shift 25% of social budget to high-intent search keywords.
