## 2. 100-Point Profile Scoring & Optimization

Audit profiles across 4 key sections:
1. **Headline (25 pts)**: Formula: *Who you help + What outcome you deliver + How you do it (Proof/Authority)*. No vague titles like "Passionate Visionary".
2. **About Section (25 pts)**:
   - Hook (first 3 lines before "see more").
   - The Problem your target ICP faces.
   - The Solution & Proof (Metrics, Case studies).
   - Clear CTA (Book a call, DM me, Website link).
3. **Featured Section (25 pts)**:
   - Must contain visual proof (Top case study, Free audit lead magnet, or High-performing post).
4. **Activity & Proof (25 pts)**:
   - Consistent weekly cadence (3-5 posts/week).
   - Active, thoughtful comments left on prospective client posts.

## 3. 7-Day High-Converting Publishing Cadence

- **Monday**: Tactical Cheatsheet / Framework (High saves & shares).
- **Tuesday**: Micro-Case Study / Customer Transformation (Builds trust).
- **Wednesday**: Contrarian Point of View / Industry Observation (Spark discussion).
- **Thursday**: Carousel / Deep Dive (High dwell time).
- **Friday**: Personal Founder Lesson / Story of Failure to Success (Human connection).
- **Weekend**: Reflection / Short high-leverage question or mindset shift.
