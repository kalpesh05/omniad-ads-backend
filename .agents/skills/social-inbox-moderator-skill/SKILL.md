# AI Unified Inbox & Comment Moderation Skill

Manage multi-channel client comment sections, eliminate spam and toxic comments, and turn prospective client comments into booked sales calls.

---

## 1. 4-Tier Comment Triage & Severity Matrix

Every inbound comment or DM must be categorized into one of 4 action buckets:

| Tier | Category | Triggers & Examples | Automated System Action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Tier 1** | **Toxic / Brand Threat** | Hate speech, profanity, competitor promotion, scam links, fake bot attacks. | **Auto-Hide / Auto-Archive**. Never engage publicly; flag for human review log. |
| **Tier 2** | **High-Intent Buying Lead** | "How much is this?", "Do you ship to Canada?", "Where can I book a call?", "DM me info". | **Priority P1 Notification**. Auto-draft helpful response with direct checkout/booking link within 5 minutes. |
| **Tier 3** | **Customer Objection / Skepticism** | "Looks overpriced", "Is this just a dropshipped product?", "Does this work for beginners?". | **Auto-Draft Empathetic Reframe**. Answer with social proof, money-back guarantee, or free trial offer. |
| **Tier 4** | **General Engagement / Praise** | "Love this!", "Saved 🔥", "Great post", fire/heart emojis. | **Engagement Boost**. Reply with warm 1-sentence response + question to keep comment thread active in the algorithm. |

---

## 2. Response Templates for High-Frequency Scenarios

### Objection: "Is this legit or a scam?"
- *Response*: "Totally understand the skepticism—the internet is full of empty promises! We've helped over [X] clients achieve [Outcome], backed by a 100% money-back guarantee. Check our verified customer case studies here: [Link]."

### High-Intent: "How much does this cost?"
- *Response*: "Hey [Name]! Plans start at $[Price] depending on your volume, and we also offer a free audit to see if we're a fit first. I've sent you a quick DM with the full breakdown!"

---

## 3. Brand Reputation Safety Guardrails
- **Never Argue with Trolls**: Arguing in comments boosts negative algorithm sentiment and damages brand prestige.
- **Rule of 1-Hour Speed**: Answering buyer inquiries within the first 60 minutes increases conversion by 391%.
