# Instant Site Audit & Lead Magnet Engine Skill

Powers the embedded `<script>` audit widget on agency websites. Ingests a client's URL and automatically generates an executive-ready 3-pillar audit report (SEO, CRO, Paid Media) with deterministic scores and high-ticket agency upsells.

---

## 1. 3-Pillar Audit Scoring Architecture (Total Score: 0–100)

When a prospect submits their URL through the embeddable audit widget, score their digital footprint across 3 pillars:

### Pillar 1: Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO & UX) — 35 Points
- **5-Second Value Prop (10 pts)**: Is the core transformation clear above the fold?
- **Call-to-Action Friction (10 pts)**: High-contrast button, single goal, clear action copy.
- **Social Proof Placement (10 pts)**: Logos, reviews, trust badges within the initial viewport.
- **Mobile Responsiveness & Speed (5 pts)**: Viewport layout, touch target spacing.

### Pillar 2: Technical & Search Visibility (SEO & Discovery) — 35 Points
- **Title Tag & Meta Description (10 pts)**: Compelling copy, keyword alignment, character limit compliance.
- **Heading Structure (H1, H2) (10 pts)**: Exactly 1 semantic H1; logical section hierarchy.
- **OpenGraph & Social Sharing Cards (10 pts)**: Valid `og:image`, `og:title` preview when shared on social.
- **Core Web Vitals Signal (5 pts)**: Fast visual load time, clean asset compression.

### Pillar 3: Paid Media Readiness & Tracking — 30 Points
- **Tracking Pixels Detected (15 pts)**: Meta Pixel, Google Tag (gtag.js), TikTok Pixel active.
- **Ad-to-Landing Page Scent (15 pts)**: Dedicated landing page vs generic homepage routing.

Want our growth engineering team to implement these fixes and scale your pipeline?
