# TikTok Viral & Short-Form Video Skill

Engineered for rapid viewer retention, TikTok Shop e-commerce conversions, and high-velocity organic virality.

---

## 1. The 3-Second Visual & Auditory Hook Triggers

On TikTok, if the viewer is not hooked in **1.8 seconds**, they swipe away. Every script must deploy one of these opening visual/auditory interrupts:

1. **The Physical Action Interrupt**: 
   - *Visual*: Dropping something, throwing an object towards the camera, or tearing open packaging.
   - *Audio*: Loud Foley sound effect + "Wait, don't buy [X] until you see this."
2. **The "Green Screen Proof" Hook**:
   - *Visual*: Host pointed at a crazy screenshot, chart, or customer review in the background.
   - *Audio*: "I thought this was fake until my order actually showed up."
3. **The "Calling Out the ICP" Hook**:
   - "If you have [hyper-specific problem, e.g. dry skin, bad posture, zero ad ROAS], this is for you."
4. **The Banned / Secret Tool Hook**:
   - "My boss is going to kill me for showing you this feature..."
5. **The Side-by-Side Split**:
   - *Visual*: Screen split 50/50 showing the \$20 alternative vs \$200 competitor.
   - *Audio*: "Testing if the TikTok viral version actually beats the original."

## 2. TikTok Shop & E-Commerce UGC Script Blueprint (30–45s)

| Timestamp | Video Section | Action & Visuals | Audio / Spoken Script |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **0:00 - 0:03** | **Visual Hook** | Problem state demonstrated with extreme emotion or close-up. | Hook Trigger (Calling out problem). |
| **0:03 - 0:08** | **Agitate Problem** | Show the pain point of existing alternatives. | "I've tried [Old Product] and it broke in 2 days..." |
| **0:08 - 0:18** | **The Discovery & Demo** | Fast-paced product demonstration in real lighting (not sterile studio). | "Then I found this on TikTok Shop, and look at how [Feature] works..." |
| **0:18 - 0:28** | **Social Proof / Value** | Show before & after, unboxing, or customer reviews. | "It already has 10,000+ 5-star reviews and it's 40% off right now." |
| **0:28 - 0:35** | **Direct Call-to-Action** | Hand pointing down towards the bottom-left corner of the phone screen. | "Tap the orange cart right down here before they sell out." |

## 3. TikTok Retention Curve Optimization

- **Pacing Rule**: Video angle or shot must change every **2.5 to 3.5 seconds**. Static talking heads die on TikTok.
- **Micro-Hooks**: At second 0:12 and 0:22, insert a micro-hook: "And wait until you see the best part...", "Here's what surprised me..."
- **Captions**: Dynamic, animated word-by-word captions centered on the safe zone (not covered by TikTok buttons or title overlay).
