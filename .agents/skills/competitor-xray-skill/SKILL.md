# Competitor X-Ray (Ad Spy & Reverse Engineering) Skill

Analyze competitor ad libraries (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center, LinkedIn Ads) to identify profitable angles, longevity signals, and creative fatigue.

---

## 1. The "Longevity-as-Signal" Principle

In performance marketing, advertisers do not pay for ads that lose money:
- **< 7 Days Active**: Testing phase / exploratory creative variant. Do not copy yet.
- **8–29 Days Active**: Validated traction; ad is performing near break-even or profitable.
- **30+ Days Active (High Signal)**: **Proven Cash Cow**. This creative format, hook angle, and offer structure is undeniably profitable for the competitor. Prioritize modeling this angle.

---

## 2. Creative Angle Clustering Framework

When evaluating a competitor's active portfolio, cluster their ads into these 5 primary creative angles:

| Angle | Visual Structure | Copy Mechanism | When Competitors Use It |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. The Problem Agitator** | Frustrated person, red text overlays, broken old workflow. | "Tired of wasting 10 hours a week on [pain]?" | Top of Funnel (cold audience awareness). |
| **2. Us vs. Them Comparison** | 2-column comparison table or split-screen video test. | Competitor has red crosses (❌); Our brand has green ticks (✅). | Middle of Funnel (consideration/switching). |
| **3. The UGC Social Proof Parade** | 3–5 rapid customer video clips stitched back-to-back. | "I was skeptical, but here is my honest review after 3 weeks..." | Retargeting & scaling trust. |
| **4. The Founder / Behind-The-Curtain** | Authentic selfie video or casual office setting. | "Why I quit my job at [Big Corp] to build this 1 tool..." | Humanizing high-ticket or SaaS offers. |
| **5. The Direct Demo & Feature Teardown** | High-speed screencast or product teardown with captions. | "Step 1: Upload. Step 2: Click once. Step 3: Done." | Low-friction impulse conversion. |

---

## 3. Reverse-Engineering Pitch Template (For Client Proposals)

When pitching prospective agency clients using competitor intelligence:
1. **Identify the Gap**: Find the competitor's 3 longest-running ads and identify what hook or objection they are ignoring.
2. **The Counter-Angle**: "Competitor X is spending \$50k/mo on Angle A, but they completely ignore [Key Customer Pain B]. Here is the 3-variant creative suite we will deploy to capture that market share."
