# YouTube Growth & Production Skill

Turn raw video ideas and transcripts into high-retention, high-CTR YouTube videos and YouTube Ad creatives without generic AI fluff.

---

## 1. 21 High-CTR Hook Formulas & Scoring System

When writing YouTube video introductions (0:00 - 0:30) or YouTube In-Stream / Shorts Ads (first 5 seconds), select one of these 21 battle-tested hook formulas:

| ID | Hook Formula | Structure | Best For |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **H1** | **The Bold Negative** | "Stop doing [common action]. It is secretly ruining your [desired outcome]." | Advice, tutorials |
| **H2** | **The High Stakes Before/After** | "In [Year 1] I was [worst state]. [Time] later, I did [extreme achievement]." | Transformation, case study |
| **H3** | **The Contrarian Truth** | "Everyone says to do [A], but after [test/experience], [A] is dead." | Breaking conventional wisdom |
| **H4** | **The Curiosity Gap** | "There is a reason [Authority/Leader] never touches [Thing]..." | Investigation, breakdowns |
| **H5** | **The Numbered Warning** | "If you notice these [X] warning signs in your [topic], run." | Urgency, problem awareness |
| **H6** | **The Specific Timeframe** | "How to achieve [desirable outcome] in [unusually short time] without [pain]." | Tactical guides |
| **H7** | **The Insider Secret** | "What [industry] insiders charge \$10,000 to teach you, in 10 minutes." | High perceived value |
| **H8** | **The Direct Challenge** | "Most people cannot pass this [metric/test]. Can you?" | Interactive, gamified |
| **H9** | **The Costly Mistake** | "This single mistake cost me \$[Amount] (so you don't have to lose it)." | Pain avoidance, financial |
| **H10**| **The Direct Demonstration** | *Show the result or climax immediately in second 0:01*, then: "Here is how it happened." | Visual or action projects |
| **H11**| **The "I Tested Everything"** | "I spent [Time/Money] testing every [Product/Method] so you don't have to." | Reviews, tier lists |
| **H12**| **The Unfair Advantage** | "This 1 setting gives [Group] an unfair advantage in [Year]." | Tool, software, growth hacks |
| **H13**| **The Hidden Mechanism** | "You think the problem is [X]. The real problem is actually [Y]." | Root-cause reframe |
| **H14**| **The Story Cold Open** | "I was standing in [place], when suddenly [shocking turning point]..." | Narrative storytelling |
| **H15**| **The Prediction** | "By [Year], [Old Tool/Way] will be obsolete. Here is what is replacing it." | Tech, trends, foresight |
| **H16**| **The Rule of 3 Contrasts** | "Bad [Niche]: [X]. Good [Niche]: [Y]. Elite [Niche]: [Z]." | Framework education |
| **H17**| **The Banned / Censored Angle** | "The [Niche] strategy that gurus try to hide from beginners." | High curiosity |
| **H18**| **The Exact Recipe** | "Steal this exact [Niche] template: Step 1, Step 2, Step 3." | Cheatsheet, templates |
| **H19**| **The "What Happens If"** | "What happens when you do [extreme thing] for 30 straight days?" | Challenge, experiment |
| **H20**| **The Inversion** | "How to guarantee failure at [Goal] (and how to invert it)." | Psychology, strategy |
| **H21**| **The 1-Second Visual Interrupt** | Immediate auditory sound effect + fast question: "Wait, look at this..." | YouTube Shorts & Ad hooks |

### Hook Scoring Rubric (Target Score: 8.5/10+)
Rate the proposed hook across 4 criteria:
1. **Curiosity or Pain (1-3 pts)**: Does it trigger immediate tension or emotional curiosity?
2. **Speed to Value (1-3 pts)**: Does it deliver the premise in under 8 seconds with zero throat-clearing?
3. **Clarity (1-2 pts)**: Can a 12-year-old understand what the video will deliver?
4. **Originality (1-2 pts)**: Does it eliminate generic AI tropes ("In this video, I'll explain...")?

## 2. Title & Thumbnail Pairing Linter

A YouTube click-surface is **Title + Thumbnail** working as an interdependent dyad.
- **Rule 1 (Zero Word Redundancy)**: Never repeat the exact same words on the thumbnail graphic as are in the video title.
- **Rule 2 (Complementary Emotion)**: 
  - If the Title asks a question, the Thumbnail must show the shocking answer or extreme reaction.
  - If the Title states a bold metric ("How I Made \$50k in 14 Days"), the Thumbnail displays the mechanism or visual proof.
- **Rule 3 (Mobile Readability)**: Thumbnail text must be **4 words or fewer**, high contrast, legible at 120px width on a phone screen.

## 3. Transcript Edit Decision List (EDL) Optimizer

When provided with a raw transcript or rough recording:
1. **Highlight Cuts ("Kill The Fluff")**:
   - Remove "throat clearing" (introductions, "welcome back to the channel", "before we get started").
   - Remove repetitive re-explanations.
   - Remove dead air or filler words ("um", "you know", "basically").
2. **Flag Retention Risers**:
   - Insert visual changes (B-roll, text overlays, graphic zooms) every **4 to 6 seconds**.
   - Insert pattern interrupts and chapter resets every **90 seconds**.

---

## 4. YouTube Shorts Repurposing Workflow

1. Scan long-form transcripts for high-energy punches (<60s duration).
2. Rewrite the first 3 seconds into a short-form hook (Formula H10, H12, or H21).
3. Ensure the script loops seamlessly: make the final sentence seamlessly connect to the opening hook.
